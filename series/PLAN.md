# Story Series Plan — 10 series × 5 parts = 50 videos

Original animated mini-series for a worldwide audience. Every part is a 45–60s Short that ends on a
cliffhanger and hides one real psychology idea inside the story (the channel's identity).
At 2 uploads a day (`.github/workflows/series.yml`), 50 parts last 25 days.

## Recurring cast ("the troupe" — same faces in every story, different roles)
| Code | Name | Look | Usual role |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Max | spiky hair, orange hoodie | the calm skeptic who explains the psychology |
| B | Leo | glasses, teal shirt | curious, dramatic, drags Max into trouble |
| C | Mira | grey bun, shawl | the wise elder who knows the secret |
| D | Bruno | red cap, moustache, overalls | the gruff worker — suspect or helper |
| E | Zara | curly hair, yellow jacket | the smart one: scientist, curator, insider |
| F | Victor | slick hair, purple suit, red tie | the rich, charming one — usually hiding something |
| V | (offscreen voice) | — | ghosts, radios, phones, whispers |

## 2 · The Tuesday That Won't End
1. **Same Day Again** — Leo wakes to the exact same Tuesday: same spilled coffee, same song. Max laughs. *Psych: déjà vu.* **Cliff:** at 11:11 PM everything snaps — Leo wakes up. "It's Tuesday."
2. **Proof** — Leo predicts every moment of Max's day. *Psych: we live mostly on autopilot habits.* **Cliff:** Max: "Wait… why do I remember yesterday too?"
3. **The Only One Who Changes** — they change everything; only barista Zara does something different each loop. **Cliff:** Zara whispers, "You noticed? This is loop forty-seven for me."
4. **The Unfinished Thing** — Zara: the loop ends when someone finishes what they left undone. *Psych: Zeigarnik effect — unfinished tasks stick in the mind.* **Cliff:** Leo realises the broken promise isn't his… it's Max's.
5. **Wednesday** — Max finally says the thing he never said; the loop breaks. **Sting:** Zara's coffee cup reads "See you Thursday."

## 3 · The App That Knows Too Much
1. **Oracle** — free app predicts Leo's day perfectly. **Cliff:** notification: "Max is lying to you."
2. **How It Knows** — Max: vague predictions feel personal. *Psych: Barnum effect.* **Cliff:** "Someone will knock in 10 minutes. Don't open."
3. **The Knock** — Victor, Oracle's charming founder, offers Leo a job. **Cliff:** Max finds the app has been listening through the mic.
4. **The Trap** — they feed it fake plans. *Psych: confirmation bias.* **Cliff:** Oracle predicts the trap… because Max's phone has it too.
5. **Exposed** — it was people + data + psychology, not magic; Leo exposes Victor live. **Sting:** app deleted… new notification: "Nice try."

## 4 · The Missing Diamond of Room 7
1. **Ten Seconds of Dark** — lights cut at a museum gala; the diamond is gone; the glass is unbroken. Suspects: curator Zara, collector Victor, guard Bruno. **Cliff:** the case is still locked.
2. **Read the Room** — *Psych: liars don't avoid eye contact more — that's a myth.* **Cliff:** camera shows Bruno in two places at once.
3. **The Loop** — footage was looped; someone hacked it. **Cliff:** the diamond is in Victor's bag!
4. **The Fake** — it's a replica. *Psych: we trust confidence over evidence.* **Cliff:** the real diamond hasn't been in the case for three years.
5. **The Trap** — Zara swapped it years ago to protect it from Victor; tonight was her trap. **Sting:** Leo finds the real diamond… in his own coat pocket.

## 5 · The Creature of Blackwood
1. **Footprints** — camping; huge footprints; a howl. **Cliff:** camera-trap photo — eyes eight feet off the ground.
2. **Past the River** — ranger Bruno: nobody crosses the river. *Psych: the unknown feels scarier than a known danger.* **Cliff:** their food bag is dragged toward a cave.
3. **The Cave** — nest, bones, still warm. **Cliff:** a giant shadow blocks the entrance.
4. **Gentle Giant** — the creature is rare and gentle — and caught in a trap. *Psych: we judge by looks first.* **Cliff:** the trap has Bruno's initials.
5. **The Real Monster** — Bruno spread monster stories to hide his poaching; they free the creature and expose him. **Sting:** last photo — two pairs of eyes.

## 6 · The Lighthouse That Lights Itself
1. **3:33 AM** — abandoned 30 years, yet it lights every night at 3:33. **Cliff:** the door is locked from inside.
2. **The Logbook** — last entry written yesterday; the keeper died 30 years ago. **Cliff:** footsteps upstairs.
3. **The Lamp Room** — a timer, a recording, fresh oil. *Psych: pareidolia — seeing faces and ghosts in noise.* **Cliff:** storm — their boat drifts away.
4. **The Keeper's Daughter** — Mira lights it because a ship was lost the night it went dark. **Cliff:** the radio: "Mayday — heading for the rocks."
5. **Guide Them Home** — they guide the ship in. **Sting:** the ship's name matches the one lost 30 years ago.

## 7 · The Last Radio Message
1. **Static** — Leo's old radio: coordinates and "If you hear this, it's 1979." **Cliff:** the coordinates are two hours away, in the desert.
2. **The Station** — an abandoned radio station. **Cliff:** the equipment is warm; the message is live.
3. **The Logbook** — a scientist vanished in 1979. **Cliff:** last entry: "Two boys will come. Tell them to look under the floor."
4. **Under the Floor** — a time capsule. *Psych: hindsight bias — "I knew it all along".* **Cliff:** a photo of Max and Leo… dated 1979.
5. **Family** — the scientist was Max's grandfather; the "boys" are Max's dad and uncle. **Sting:** the radio crackles: "If you hear this, it's 2049."

## 8 · The Night Train
1. **Last Train** — passengers vanish after every tunnel. **Cliff:** Max and Leo are the only ones left; it won't stop.
2. **The Conductor** — Victor: "No stops until you answer the question." **Cliff:** "What did you lose?"
3. **The Mirror Carriage** — a carriage with copies of themselves. *Psych: misdirection — attention is easy to steer.* **Cliff:** one copy blinks out of sync.
4. **The Trick** — it's an immersive mystery show Leo booked half-asleep. **Cliff:** one "actor" isn't on the cast list.
5. **Final Stop** — they escape and laugh. **Sting:** in the farewell photo, the extra passenger is still standing behind them.

## 9 · The Frozen Station
1. **The Storm** — mountain research station; supplies vanish. **Cliff:** the radio cable is cut.
2. **Blame** — *Psych: under stress, groups look for a scapegoat.* **Cliff:** Bruno walks into the storm and disappears.
3. **The Ice Cave** — footprints lead to a giant shape frozen in ice. **Cliff:** the ice cracks… from inside.
4. **Zara's Secret** — she knew, and hid it for her career. **Cliff:** the frozen eye opens.
5. **Thaw** — the cracking is the glacier, the creature is an ancient animal, and Bruno was saving the supplies from the avalanche. **Sting:** the eye blinks again.

## 10 · Lost in the Deep
1. **The Wreck** — diving for a legendary wreck with Captain Mira. **Cliff:** the anchor line is cut.
2. **Breathe** — *Psych: panic burns oxygen — slow breathing keeps you alive.* **Cliff:** something huge glides past.
3. **The Giant** — a giant squid circles them. **Cliff:** the wreck door opens by itself.
4. **Inside** — treasure and a captain's last letter. **Cliff:** oxygen alarm.
5. **The Surface** — they escape; the real treasure is the letter that solves the wreck's mystery. **Sting:** on the boat, wet footprints that aren't theirs.

## What makes each part shareable (checked with `npm run check:series`)
- **Hook headline on screen** for the whole video (not "Part 1/5") — it also leads the YouTube title.
  Curiosity first, series name and part number last: `Never answer if someone calls your name after 9 PM | Don't Answer After 9 Pt 1`.
- **First line lands in ~1 second** — mid-action, no greetings, no recap longer than one line.
- **Under a minute** — most parts are 25–50s; short parts get rewatched, which Shorts rewards.
- **A cut every line** (wide / close-up), shake on shocks, title cards for time jumps.
- **Cliffhanger last line + "WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?" end card** — pushes follows and next-part views.
- **Comment bait** — mysteries invite guesses ("who is the grey coat?"), finales ask "Season 2?".
- **One real psychology idea per part** — the reason to watch even if you skip the story.

Honest limits: no script can promise views. What decides it is the first 2 seconds and how many people
watch to the end — check YouTube Studio's "stayed to watch" for the first few parts and rewrite hooks that lose people.

## Writing rules (every part)
- 12–16 lines, 45–60 seconds; first line is a hook, not a greeting. Part 2+ opens with a one-line recap.
- One psychology idea per part, said in one or two plain sentences.
- Last line = cliffhanger. Final part = payoff + a sting for "Season 2?".
- Simple English for a global audience; no country-specific slang or references.
